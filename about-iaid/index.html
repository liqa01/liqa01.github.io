About IAID

A professional society for international doctors in Ireland

IAID provides a formal, constructive, and supportive platform for international doctors contributing to patient care, medical education, and healthcare delivery across Ireland.

Irish Association of International Doctors (IAID) is a professional medical society supporting doctors who have trained internationally and are working, training, settling, or preparing to contribute within the Irish healthcare system.

IAID provides a constructive, credible, and collaborative platform for education, mentorship, advocacy, professional development, immigration and relocation signposting, research, networking, and community support.

Who we are

IAID is a member-focused professional society built around service, dignity, fairness, and practical support. It brings together international doctors across specialties, grades, counties, and career stages, while maintaining a formal and respectful voice in public and stakeholder discussions.

Who we support

IAID supports newly arriving International Medical Graduates, non-consultant hospital doctors, doctors seeking training or professional progression, doctors navigating registration and immigration processes, doctors relocating with families, and doctors seeking a stronger professional network in Ireland.

Why IAID exists

International doctors make an essential contribution to patient care, hospital services, community medicine, and the resilience of the Irish health system. IAID exists to help identify shared challenges, offer reliable signposting, encourage mentoring, support integration, and represent international doctors through respectful dialogue with healthcare stakeholders.

Our approach

IAID's approach is professional, diplomatic, evidence-informed, and non-confrontational. The society aims to work with hospitals, training bodies, regulators, policymakers, media, and community partners to improve understanding and support practical progress.

Our role in the Irish healthcare system

IAID seeks to be a positive partner in Ireland's healthcare landscape by supporting doctors, strengthening professional connection, encouraging retention, and contributing informed perspectives to discussions on training, workforce planning, immigration processes, and workplace dignity.

Leadership & Organisational Structure

IAID is supported by a growing leadership structure comprising executive officers, secretariat members, functional committee leads, advisory representatives, and county chairs. This structure helps coordinate education, advocacy, relocation support, media engagement, community representation, and local peer support for international doctors across Ireland.

Our Values

Dignity

Recognising the professional worth, lived experience, and personal dignity of every doctor while keeping patient care at the centre of our work.

Fairness

Supporting transparent pathways, fair access to opportunity, and consistent treatment in professional, training, and administrative processes.

Inclusion

Building a welcoming society where doctors from diverse backgrounds can participate, contribute, and feel part of the Irish medical community.

Professionalism

Maintaining a measured, evidence-informed, respectful, and constructive tone in member support, advocacy, and public communication.

Collaboration

Working with hospitals, professional bodies, regulators, policymakers, community organisations, and media to support shared understanding.

Service

Helping doctors access guidance, connection, and support so they can continue serving patients, colleagues, and communities with confidence.

Excellence

Encouraging high standards in education, research, leadership, professional development, and compassionate clinical practice.
