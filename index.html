Who IAID Supports 01 People & partnership Doctors, newcomers, and healthcare partners across Ireland

A professional society for international doctors in Ireland

IAID provides a structured, professional platform for International Medical Graduates and international doctors working across Ireland. The association brings together doctors from diverse backgrounds to support professional growth, improve access to guidance, strengthen peer networks, and promote constructive engagement with healthcare stakeholders.

01

International doctors

Supporting doctors navigating clinical work, career development, training pathways, relocation, and professional life in Ireland.

02

Newly arriving doctors

Providing practical orientation, peer support, and signposting for doctors beginning their journey in the Irish healthcare system.

03

Healthcare stakeholders

Promoting constructive dialogue with hospitals, training bodies, policymakers, media, and professional organisations.

Mission

Support, represent, mentor, and empower

To support, represent, mentor, and empower international doctors in Ireland through education, advocacy, professional development, immigration guidance, relocation support, networking, and community support.

Vision

A united community shaping the future

To build a united, empowered community of international doctors contributing positively to the Irish healthcare system and shaping the future of medical training in Ireland.

Key Areas of Work 02 Seven priorities Guidance, representation, professional growth, and community

Practical support, professional development, advocacy, and community building

IAID's work is organised around clear areas of support so doctors can find guidance, connection, and representation at different stages of their professional journey.

Education & Training

Guidance on postgraduate training pathways, career progression, educational sessions, webinars, and mentorship opportunities.

Media & Representation

Responsible public communication, constructive engagement with stakeholders, and professional representation of international doctors.

Research & Professional Development

Encouraging academic collaboration, research participation, leadership development, and professional excellence.

Immigration Advisory

General guidance and signposting on visa pathways, regulatory processes, and policies affecting international doctors and their families.

Relocation Support

Practical support for newly arriving doctors, including orientation around hospitals, accommodation, community integration, and settling in Ireland.

Events & Networking

Professional, educational, and social events that strengthen collaboration and peer support across the medical community.

Community Support

Local support through county representatives, hospital contacts, peer networks, and a respectful professional community.

Advocacy and Public Engagement

Advocacy rooted in fairness, dignity, and workforce sustainability

IAID continues a long-standing tradition of constructive advocacy for international doctors in Ireland. Its work focuses on professional fairness, training access, immigration stability, family wellbeing, workplace dignity, and sustainable healthcare workforce planning.

View Advocacy Themes

Training access and progression

Supporting fairer access to postgraduate training, career pathways, and professional recognition.

Immigration and Stamp 4 reform

Highlighting the importance of stable immigration pathways for doctors who contribute to the Irish healthcare system.

Work permit and visa policy

Promoting practical policy discussion around work permits, family reunification, and administrative delays.

Workplace dignity

Raising awareness of bullying, discrimination, racism, and the importance of safe, respectful workplaces.

Healthcare workforce retention

Contributing to discussion on retaining skilled international doctors within the Irish health service.

Our Story 03 Advocacy into action Seven years of national advocacy carried forward by IAID

From Train Us For Ireland to IAID

IAID emerges from a seven-year national advocacy journey that began under the banner "Train Us For Ireland". Today, IAID carries that work forward as a professional society focused on support, mentorship, advocacy, education, representation, and community building.

01

Train Us For Ireland

A national advocacy movement giving voice to issues affecting International Medical Graduates.

02

Seven-year advocacy journey

Sustained professional dialogue, public awareness, and stakeholder engagement on training, immigration, dignity, and retention.

03

IAID professional society

A formal platform for support, mentorship, education, representation, and community building.

04

Future direction

Constructive collaboration with doctors and healthcare stakeholders to strengthen international doctor support in Ireland.
