Why register interest

Registering interest helps IAID understand member needs, plan educational events, develop committee work, identify regional priorities, and keep doctors informed as the society grows.

Who can register interest

International doctors in Ireland, newly arriving IMGs, doctors preparing for registration, doctors in training, NCHDs, specialists, and supporters of IAID's professional objectives can register interest.

Benefits of joining

  • Professional updates, educational sessions, and training pathway signposting.
  • Mentorship, peer support, and connection with doctors across Ireland.
  • Opportunities to contribute to committees, research, events, and community initiatives.
  • Networking across counties, hospitals, practices, specialties, and career stages.
  • General signposting on relocation, immigration, professional development, and useful official resources.

How members can contribute

Members can support IAID by sharing professional experience, mentoring colleagues, joining committees, helping organise events, contributing to research and resources, or supporting regional networks in a way that suits their capacity and expertise.
